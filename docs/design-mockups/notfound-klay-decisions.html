Not found — Klay's first empty room

Audit found four "it isn't here" surfaces: the 404 route, conversation-not-found (ChatPage), workspace-not-found (a dead end — no action at all), and the tiny card-not-found chip in chat (staying text-only: in-flow, repeatable, too small for the coarse grid). One semantic moment, currently three dialects. Klay's look animation is the unifier — he's a 2-frame mockup here; the app uses the real PixelKlay sprites.

Recommendation

  • 1B + 2B. One "lost" moment, one animation (look), two sizes. The 404 gets the headline treatment; in-app states get the quiet one. C is a great gag but spends the layout on it.
  • The card-not-found chip in chat stays text-only — copy nudge at most ("This card was deleted"). Three Klay-lost surfaces is already the right dose; a fourth in every stale transcript would overexpose him.
  • Implementation: NotFoundState component (title, body, action, klayScale), used at three call sites; 404 keeps its full-page wrapper.
