Klay on the dashboard

First, the tension worth naming: Klay's rule is "rare, meaningful moments — keep him scarce." The dashboard is the one surface you hit every session. So the only version worth doing is one where he earns the spot — and there's a hook already sitting there.

Your greeting already rotates by time of day — morning / afternoon / evening / night, three lines each. Today a generic pink Sparkle announces it. Klay has animations that map onto exactly those four moods.

B · RECOMMENDED

Klay replaces the Sparkle — and tells the time

A swap, not an addition — no new weight on the page. And he's never the same twice, so he reads as a living detail rather than a mascot parked on your home screen. This is the whole idea: the greeting says the time of day in words, Klay says it in posture.

My take

B — but only because of the time-of-day hook. A static Klay on the home screen would be exactly the wallpaper his rule warns about. Tied to getGreetingSlot(), he stops being decoration and becomes the only thing on the page that knows what time it is. He's a companion who's awake when you are and asleep when you should be. That's worth a daily appearance; a mascot standing there is not.

  • Scale 4 (48px) — a hair larger than the 32px Sparkle, small enough that the chat input stays the hero.
  • Zero new state — getGreetingSlot(new Date().getHours()) already exists and already drives the words.
  • The real cost: the Sparkle marks "AI lives here" on your most AI-forward surface. Swapping it trades an AI signal for a product signal. If that trade bothers you, A is a perfectly good answer and Klay stays rare.

C and D both fail for the same reason: anything above ~48px in that slot starts arguing with the chat input, and the chat input is the dashboard. E is two mascots in one greeting.

Reply B to build it (4 animations wired to the existing time slots) — or A to leave the dashboard alone and keep him scarce.
