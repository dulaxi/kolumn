---
title: Export your data, switch themes, reduce motion
---

Three settings worth knowing where to find, in one pass: getting a copy of everything you've made, picking how the app looks, and turning animation down if it bothers you.

## Export everything you own

**Settings → Privacy → Export** downloads a JSON file — `kolumn-backup-YYYY-MM-DD.json` — with every board, column, and card you have. It's a one-time snapshot, not a live sync: nothing updates it after you download it, and nothing about your account changes when you do. What exactly is (and isn't) in the file is covered in [How do I export or delete my data?](/support/export-or-delete-your-data).

## Pick a theme

**Settings → General → Preferences → Appearance** has three options: **System**, **Light**, and **Dark**. System follows your OS's own light/dark setting and switches live if you change it there. Pick Light or Dark instead if you want Kolumn to stay put no matter what the OS is doing.

## Turn animation down

**Settings → General → Accessibility → Motion** has the same three-way shape: **System**, **Full**, and **Reduced**. This is a second, independent trigger layered on top of your OS's own reduce-motion setting:

- **System** follows whatever your OS has set, live — if your OS is already set to reduce motion, Kolumn respects that without you touching this control.
- **Reduced** turns Kolumn's animation down regardless of what the OS says.
- **Full** keeps animation on even if your OS asks apps to reduce motion.

Use System if you've already set a preference at the OS level and want Kolumn to follow it. Use Reduced or Full if you want Kolumn's own answer to be different from your OS's — a shared or work computer you don't control the system setting on, for instance.
