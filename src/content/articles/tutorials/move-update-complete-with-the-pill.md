---
title: Move, update, and complete cards by typing
---

The pill doesn't just create cards — on Pro, it edits the ones already on the board. This tutorial covers moving, updating, and completing cards by typing instead of dragging. It needs Pro: on Free, the pill's AI side only creates cards, and asking it to move or update an existing one won't do anything.

## Step 1 — Move a card

Open a board that has at least one card in a column other than where you want it. Click the pill and type:

```
Move the venue booking card to In Progress
```

Press Enter. The pill sends this to the AI, which finds "the venue booking card" on the board you're viewing and moves it. You'll see a short confirmation line like "Moved 'Book the venue'".

## Step 2 — Update a few fields at once

Type a request that changes more than one thing:

```
Set the venue card's priority to high and give it a due date of this Friday
```

The AI updates only the fields you named — priority and due date here — and leaves everything else on the card alone. Nothing is replaced unless you mention it.

## Step 3 — Mark something complete

```
Mark the venue booking as done
```

The card is flagged complete. It stays in whatever column it's already in — completing a card is a status change, not a move. Ask to "uncomplete" or "mark it not done" and it flips back.

## Step 4 — Act on several cards at once

The same instructions work in bulk when you describe a group instead of one title:

```
Move everything in To Do to In Progress
```

Every matching card moves in one request. The same applies to updates and completion — "mark all the cards in Done as complete" works just as well as naming one card.

## Step 5 — Delete a card

```
Delete the venue booking card
```

The card disappears immediately and a 5-second Undo toast appears, the same as deleting by hand. If you meant to keep it, hit Undo before it expires — after that it's gone.

## What else Pro's pill can do

The steps above cover the common cases. The same board-scoped pill can also duplicate a card, check or uncheck specific checklist items, add or delete a column, rename the board or change its icon, and invite or remove a workspace member — see [What the pill can do](/support/what-the-pill-can-do) for the full list.

## Limits worth knowing

Every action here is scoped to the board you're on — the pill can't reach a card on a different board, and it can't create a new board. Removing a workspace member has no undo, so the AI checks with you in its reply first. And all of this is Pro-only; on Free the pill can create cards but not edit or remove them — see [Free vs Pro](/support/free-vs-pro).
