---
title: Share a single board
---

Sometimes one person needs one board — a client, a contractor, someone outside your team — and nothing more. This tutorial shares a single board with someone by email, with no workspace involved, and walks through what shows up on their side too.

For the short answer plus edge cases (removing someone, what leaving does to their assigned cards), see the support article [Share a single board](/support/share-a-single-board). This tutorial is the walkthrough.

## Step 1 — Open Share

Open the board you want to share. At the top, on the right side of the toolbar, click **Share**. A panel opens with an email field and an **Invite** button, and below that, a **Members** list with just you on it, marked Owner.

## Step 2 — Send the invite

Type your collaborator's email and click **Invite**. The field clears, a confirmation toast appears, and their email drops into a new **Pending invitations** section in the same panel — that's your receipt that it sent, and where you'd cancel it if you needed to.

## Step 3 — What they see when they sign in

Once your collaborator signs in, they'll notice a badge on the notification bell at the bottom-left of the sidebar (next to their account menu). Opening it shows the invitation as its own row, with the board's name and an inline **Accept** / **Decline**.

## Step 4 — They accept

Clicking **Accept** adds the board immediately. It now shows up under **Shared with me** in their sidebar, and opening it looks like any other board — same cards, same columns, same everything you see — except they can't share it further or remove other members (that's owner-only).

## What they can't do

A board member can see and work the cards, but they can't invite anyone else to the board, remove other members, or delete it — those stay owner-only. If they decide they don't want access anymore, they can leave from the same Share panel, now labeled **Members** on their side.
