---
title: Find anything with ⌘K
---

You don't need to remember which board a card is on. Open search, type a few letters, press Enter. That's the whole workflow — this walks through it once, plus what's worth knowing before you rely on it.

## Step 1 — Open search

Press **⌘K** (Mac) or **Ctrl+K** (everyone else) from anywhere in the app. `/` does the same thing, in case your fingers land there first.

## Step 2 — Just start typing

Type part of a card's title, part of its description, or its task number (`42` matches `#42`). Results start appearing at two characters, and every board you're a member of is searched at once — you don't need to know which board the card is on before you look for it.

## Step 3 — Get to the card without touching the mouse

Use **↓** and **↑** to move down and up the result list, then **Enter** to open the highlighted one. Kolumn switches you to that card's board and opens the card itself, not just the board it's on. Clicking a result does the same thing, if you'd rather.

## Step 4 — Back out

**Esc** closes search and puts you back where you were. Search only finds — nothing you type there creates or changes a card.

## Two things that trip people up

- **Archived cards don't show up.** If you're sure a card exists but search comes up empty, check whether it was archived.
- **The first search after opening Kolumn can be a beat slower.** Opening search loads any board's cards it hasn't fetched yet, so on a workspace with a lot of boards, the very first search of a session may take a moment before everything's included.

## Worth memorizing alongside it

**⌘B** (or **Ctrl+B**) collapses the sidebar when you want a board to have the full width, and **Shift+?** pops up the shortcut list if you forget one mid-session. The full reference — every shortcut Kolumn binds, plus what search actually matches — is in [Keyboard shortcuts and search](/support/keyboard-shortcuts-and-search).
