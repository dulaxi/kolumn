---
title: Ask your boards a question
---

Chat is the place to ask about your boards instead of acting on them. This tutorial has you ask three kinds of questions and see what comes back. It works the same on Free and Pro — chat's lookups aren't gated by tier, only the [daily message count](/support/daily-limit) is.

## Step 1 — Start a conversation

Open `/chat` and click "New chat". Type:

```
What's overdue this week?
```

Send it. While the AI is working you'll see a short activity note appear inline — something like "Searched cards · 4 results" — before the answer streams in. That note means the AI looked your cards up rather than guessing; it searches across every board you're a member of, not just one.

## Step 2 — Ask about one board

Start a new chat and ask:

```
Summarize the Marketing board
```

This time the activity note reads "Summarized Marketing". The answer covers the board's columns in order, what's in each, and totals like how many cards are complete or overdue — a structured snapshot, not a guess from memory.

## Step 3 — Ask about one card

```
What's the status of the venue booking card?
```

The AI looks up that one card and answers from its actual description, checklist, and due date. If more than one card shares that title across your boards, it asks which board before answering.

## Step 4 — Watch the right-hand rail

As cards come up in the conversation — searched, summarized, or looked up — they collect in a rail alongside the chat. Click one to open it directly, without leaving the conversation.

## What chat won't do

Ask chat to move, edit, or delete something and it won't — there's no write tool wired to this surface at all, on any plan. For that, go to the board and use the pill instead: see [Turn a list into cards with the pill](/tutorials/list-to-cards-with-the-pill) and [Move, update, and complete cards by typing](/tutorials/move-update-complete-with-the-pill).

## Limits worth knowing

Chat draws from the same 20-messages-a-day allowance as the pill, if you're on Free. Pro removes that limit but doesn't add anything chat can do beyond asking and looking up — see [Chat vs the pill](/support/chat-vs-the-pill).
