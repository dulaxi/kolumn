---
title: Set up a workspace and invite your team
---

A workspace groups your team's boards under one roof — everyone you add can see every board in it and gets assigned to cards on any of them. This tutorial creates one, invites a teammate, and shows what happens on both sides once they join.

## Step 1 — Create the workspace

Open the workspace switcher at the top of the sidebar and click **Create workspace**. Give it a name — your team or department is usually the right call — and pick a color for its icon from the palette. Click **Create**.

You land on the workspace's own page: a header with its name and color, a **Members** section (just you, marked Owner, so far), and an **Invite members** section below it.

## Step 2 — Invite a teammate

In **Invite members**, type their email address and click **Send invite**. It moves into a **Pending invitations** list underneath, showing the email and that it's still pending. If you change your mind, click the invitation to cancel it — nothing happens on their end either way until they accept.

Inviting doesn't require them to already have a Kolumn account. If they don't, they'll sign up first; either way, the invitation is waiting for them the next time they're signed in.

## Step 3 — What your teammate sees

Once they sign in, they'll notice a small badge on the Workspace section of the sidebar — that's the pending invitation. Opening the Workspaces page shows it as a card with **Accept** and **Decline**. Accepting adds them as a member immediately: every board in the workspace shows up for them right away, and they can be assigned to cards on any of them.

## Step 4 — See live sync in action

With your teammate now a member, open any board in the workspace on your screen and have them open the same one on theirs. Create a card, or drag one between columns — it appears on their screen within moments, no refresh needed. This is the same live sync that keeps every board in the workspace current for everyone in it, all the time, not just during a demo.

## What membership doesn't do

Joining a workspace doesn't grant access to boards outside it — your personal boards, or another workspace's boards, stay exactly as private as before. And there's no partial access inside a workspace: everyone in it sees everything in it.

## Next
