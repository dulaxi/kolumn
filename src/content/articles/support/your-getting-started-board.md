title: Your getting-started board
---

The first time you sign in, Kolumn seeds a board called **Welcome** for you. It's a working board, not a video — every card teaches you one thing by having you do it. It's seeded once; it won't come back if you delete it.

## What's on it

**To do**

- **Welcome to Kolumn** — an overview card with a checklist. Its second item is "Drag me to In progress" — do that first to see drag-and-drop work.
- **Try labels** — open the card, click the dashed **Label** button, type a name, and press Enter.
- **Set a due date** — click the date pill on the card and pick a day. Due dates change color: overdue turns copper, today turns honey, anything upcoming stays lime.
- **Add a checklist** — open the card and add a few items to see checklist items tick off.
- **Ask AI for help** — explains the two AI surfaces: the pill at the bottom of the board for actions, and Chat in the sidebar for questions.

**In progress**

- **Click my icon to flip its style** — card icons have two looks, a boxed 40×40 icon and a plain bare icon. Clicking either one flips every card on the board between the two styles.
- **Click my /style label to flip its style** — labels render three ways (as text, as an outlined pill, or as a color dot); clicking any label cycles through the three, board-wide.
- **Make this board yours** — rename a column from its **⋯** menu, and click the board's icon in the sidebar to pick a new one.

**Done**

- **You're ready** — already marked complete, showing what a finished card looks like.

## When you're done with it

The Welcome board is a real board — nothing about it is protected. Delete it from its menu in the sidebar once you don't need it anymore, or leave it in place as a cheat sheet. Deleting it gives you the same 5-second undo as any other delete; see [Undo a delete](/support/undo-a-delete).

## Starting your real work

Use **New board** in the sidebar any time to create a blank board with your own columns. Starter templates tailored to your role are offered once, during onboarding — see [Create a board from a template](/support/create-a-board-from-a-template).
