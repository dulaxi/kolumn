---
title: Create a workspace and invite members
---

A workspace is a container for a team's boards. Everyone you add to it can see every board inside it and can be assigned to cards on any of them — it's the option to reach for when a group of people should share everything, not just one board.

If you only need to give one or two people access to a single board, you don't need a workspace at all — see [Share a single board](/support/share-a-single-board) for the lighter-weight option and how the two systems differ.

## Create one

Open the workspace switcher at the top of the sidebar and click **Create workspace** (the same action is also available as **New workspace** from the Workspaces page if you don't have one yet). Give it a name and pick a color for its icon, then confirm. You're automatically its owner and its first member.

## Invite people

From the workspace's page, the **Invite members** section takes an email address. Only the owner sees this — members don't get an invite form of their own. Send the invite and it appears under **Pending invitations** until it's accepted or you cancel it.

An invitation doesn't add someone automatically. The person has to be signed in to Kolumn and accept it themselves: after signing in, they'll see the pending invitation (a badge on the Workspace section of the sidebar, and a card to accept or decline on the Workspaces page). Accepting adds them as a member immediately; declining removes the invitation with no other effect.

## What membership means

Once someone joins, they can see every board in the workspace and can be assigned to cards on any of them. There's no per-board permission inside a workspace — membership is all-or-nothing for the boards that live there. If you need to keep a workspace board private from one member, it needs to live outside the workspace instead.

The owner can remove a member at any time from the workspace's member list. Removing someone revokes their access to every board in the workspace immediately, and any card they were assigned to on those boards has their name cleared from it automatically — the assignment doesn't linger as a name nobody can act on.
