---
title: Leave or remove someone from a board
---

This covers board membership specifically — one board's Share/Members list, not a workspace. See [Share a single board](/support/share-a-single-board) for how someone gets added in the first place.

## If you're the owner: remove a member

Open the board and click **Share**. Hover a member's row and click the trash icon next to their name. This removes them immediately — there's no confirmation step, so double-check the row before you click. You can't remove the owner (that's you), and there's no way to remove yourself this way either; see below for that.

## If you're a member: leave

Open the board and click **Members**. Your own row shows a **Leave** action. Confirming shows a warning first — "You'll lose access to this board. The owner can re-invite you later." — and then removes you right away.

## If you're the owner and want out

Owners can't leave their own board. There's no ownership-transfer feature — the only way to stop being responsible for a board you own is to delete it outright, which removes it (and everything on it) for everyone with access, not just you.

## What happens to assigned cards

Removing someone — whether they leave or you remove them — clears their name from every card on that board where they were the assignee. This happens automatically as part of removing their membership; you don't need to go find and clear the cards yourself. The card itself isn't touched otherwise — it keeps its title, description, and everything else, just without an assignee.

One edge case worth knowing: this matches on the person's display name, not a hidden ID. If you'd separately typed in a free-text assignee (someone without a Kolumn account) whose name happens to match a member who just left, that free-text assignment gets cleared too. You'd need to re-add it by typing the name again.
