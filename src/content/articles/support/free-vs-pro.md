---
title: Free vs Pro
---

Free and Pro are the same product — every board, every drag-and-drop action, every template. What changes between them is only what the AI is allowed to do.

## Free

- Unlimited boards, columns, and cards, with realtime sync and templates included
- The pill can create cards from plain language, plus split pasted lists into cards instantly (that path never uses the AI at all)
- 20 AI messages a day, shared between the pill and chat — see [Why did the AI say I hit my daily limit?](/support/daily-limit)
- Chat's full read side — ask questions, search your cards, and get a board summary — works the same as on Pro, with no extra limit beyond the daily message count

## Pro

- No daily AI message limit
- The pill can also move, update, complete, duplicate, and delete cards, manage checklists, add or delete columns, rename or delete the board, and invite or remove workspace members — see [What the pill can do](/support/what-the-pill-can-do)
- Priced at ${{PRICING.limits.proMonthlyUsd}} a month

Pro is framed with a 7-day trial, and Kolumn's paid billing hasn't launched yet — choosing Pro from Settings or onboarding switches your account over immediately, at no charge, with no payment method collected anywhere in the app. See the [pricing page](/pricing) for the current terms, or go straight to [Upgrade to Pro](/upgrade/pro).

## What doesn't change

Chat is read-only on both plans — it never creates, moves, or deletes anything, no matter your tier. And every plan keeps the same data: workspaces, sharing, export, and account deletion aren't gated by tier at all.

## Switching later

Move between plans any time from Settings. Downgrading to Free keeps every board and card exactly as it is; only what the AI can do next changes.
