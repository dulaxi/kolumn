---
title: Move, reorder, and complete cards
---

Cards move by dragging, in the classic kanban way, plus one click to mark them done.

## Move a card to another column

Press and drag a card into a different column, then release. Drop it above, below, or between existing cards to control where it lands — the card snaps into that position, not just to the top or bottom.

## Reorder cards within a column

Drag a card up or down inside its own column to change its order there. This only reflects a manual order when the board's Sort control (in the board header, next to Filter) is set to **Manual** — that's the default. Switch it to Due date, Priority, Created, or Alphabetical and the column displays in that order instead; dragging to reorder only makes sense under Manual sort.

## Mark a card complete

Click the check-circle next to a card's title. The title gets a strikethrough and the check turns lime. Click it again to reopen the card. Completing a card doesn't move it to another column or out of the board — it stays exactly where it is, just marked done.

## On touch devices

Press and hold a card briefly before dragging — this avoids turning an ordinary scroll into an accidental drag on a touchscreen.
