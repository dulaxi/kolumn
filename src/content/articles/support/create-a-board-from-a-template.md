---
title: Create a board from a template
---

Kolumn offers starter board templates once, during onboarding, and a blank-board option you can use any time from the sidebar.

## Starter templates (onboarding)

On the "What kind of work do you do?" step of onboarding, pick your role from the dropdown — Engineering, Design, Product management, Marketing, Operations, Sales, Founder / leadership, Student, or Something else. Picking a role reveals two or three starter boards suited to it (for example, Engineering offers a sprint board, a bug-triage flow, and a release roadmap). Click one and Kolumn creates it immediately — columns, starter cards, checklists, and priorities already filled in, ready to edit.

If none of the starters fit, click **I have my own topic** to skip straight to your dashboard with no board created yet.

This is a one-time step. There's currently no way to browse or apply a starter template again later from inside the app.

## Starting blank

Any time, click **New board** in the sidebar. You'll set:

- **Name** — required.
- **Icon** — optional, pick one from the icon picker.
- **Columns** — four editable fields, pre-filled with To Do, In Progress, Review, and Done. Change any of them, or leave the defaults — you can rename, add, or delete columns later regardless. See [Add, rename, and delete columns](/support/add-rename-and-delete-columns).

Click **Create board** and it opens right away, empty and ready for cards.
