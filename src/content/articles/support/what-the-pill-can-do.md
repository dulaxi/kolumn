---
title: What the pill can do
---

Every board in Kolumn has a pill at the bottom — a text field that reads "Type a task or paste notes...". Type into it and Kolumn either turns your text into cards instantly, or hands it to the AI to act on the board you're currently looking at.

## It only ever touches the board you're on

The pill is pinned to whatever board you have open. It can't reach into another board, and it can't create a brand-new board — that's a chat or dashboard action, not something the pill does.

## What both plans can do, no AI required

Paste a list with commas or line breaks and Kolumn splits it into cards on the spot. This fast path never calls the AI, so it doesn't touch your daily message count on Free. See [Turn a list into cards with the pill](/tutorials/list-to-cards-with-the-pill).

## What the Free plan's AI can do

Type a plain-language request instead of a list — "add a card to follow up with the venue, due Friday, high priority" — and the pill sends it to the AI, which creates one card and fills in what it can infer: title, description, priority, due date, labels, checklist, assignee. On Free, creating cards is the only thing the AI side of the pill does. Each request counts as one of your [20 AI messages a day](/support/daily-limit).

## What Pro adds

Pro removes the daily message limit and unlocks the rest of the pill's toolbox, still scoped to the current board:

- Move one card, or a batch of cards, to a different column
- Update a card's title, description, priority, labels, checklist, assignee, or due date — one at a time or in bulk
- Mark cards complete, or uncomplete them
- Duplicate a card
- Check or uncheck specific checklist items
- Delete a card, a column, or the board itself
- Add or delete a column
- Rename the board or change its icon
- Invite or remove a workspace member

Walk through these in [Move, update, and complete cards by typing](/tutorials/move-update-complete-with-the-pill).

## What the pill never does, on either plan

- **Search across boards, summarize one, or look up a single card's full detail.** Those live in chat, not the pill — see [Chat vs the pill](/support/chat-vs-the-pill).
- **Create a new board.**
- **Act on a board other than the one you have open.**

## Deletes get an undo, not a confirmation dialog

Deleting a card, column, or board through the pill happens right away and drops a 5-second Undo toast, exactly like deleting by hand. Removing a workspace member can't be undone, so the AI is instructed to check with you in its reply before doing that one.

## When a multi-step request only partly works

One message can chain several steps — "create three cards, then move them to In Progress." If a step along the way fails, Kolumn tells you which ones landed and which didn't instead of staying quiet about it.
