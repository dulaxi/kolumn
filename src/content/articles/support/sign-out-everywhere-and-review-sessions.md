---
title: Sign out everywhere and review sessions
---

`Settings → Account` shows every device currently signed in to your account, and gives you two different ways to sign out — this one device, or all of them at once.

## Review your active sessions

The **Active sessions** table lists device, an approximate location, when it was created, and when it was last active. Location comes from the session's IP address on a best-effort basis — it can be approximate, or show as unavailable if it can't be resolved. Your current session is marked **Current** and has no actions on it; every other row has a kebab menu with **Revoke session**, which ends that session immediately.

## Sign out of just this device

`Settings → Account → Sign out` ends your session on the device you're using right now. Every other session you're signed into elsewhere keeps working.

## Sign out of everywhere

`Settings → Account → Log out of all devices` ends every session on your account, including the one you're using — you'll be signed out immediately too, not just the other devices. Use this if you've lost a device, left yourself signed in somewhere you shouldn't be, or just want a clean slate.

## What this doesn't do

Signing out — from one device or all of them — doesn't touch your account, your boards, or your data. It only ends sessions. If you're trying to remove access entirely, that's [account deletion](/support/export-or-delete-your-data), a separate and permanent action.
