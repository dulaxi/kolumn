---
title: Undo a delete
---

Deleting a card, a column, or a board doesn't happen immediately — you get a 5-second window to change your mind first.

## How it works

The moment you delete something, it disappears from your screen right away and a toast appears in the message stack: "Task deleted — undo?", "Section deleted — undo?", or "Board deleted — undo?" depending on what you deleted. A small circular-arrow icon appears in that toast — click it within 5 seconds and the delete is cancelled; whatever you deleted comes right back.

If you don't click it, the toast disappears after 5 seconds and the delete goes through for real at that point — it's not reversible after that.

## What gets an undo

- **Cards** — deleting a card from its detail view (the trash icon) goes through this same 5-second window.
- **Columns** — deleting a column deletes every card inside it too. If the column had cards, Kolumn asks you to confirm first and tells you how many tasks are about to go; confirm, and then it's the same instant removal and 5-second undo toast as anything else. An empty column deletes straight away, no confirmation step.
- **Boards** — deleting a board from its menu in the sidebar always asks you to confirm first (it warns that the board and all its tasks will be deleted). Confirm, and the board disappears from the sidebar immediately with the same 5-second undo toast.

## What doesn't get undo

Marking a card complete, archiving a card, and removing a label are immediate — there's no undo toast for those. Archived cards aren't deleted, though; they move to the board's Archived view and can be restored from there at any time, no time limit.
