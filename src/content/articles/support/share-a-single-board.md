---
title: Share a single board
---

Every board has its own **Share** button, and it works on its own — you don't need a workspace to use it. This is board sharing: a separate, lighter system from [workspaces](/support/create-a-workspace-and-invite-members), for when one board needs another person on it and the rest of your boards don't.

## Invite someone

Open the board and click **Share** at the top of it (a board you don't own shows the same button labeled **Members** instead — see below). Enter their email and click **Invite**. It appears under **Pending invitations** until they accept.

Like a workspace invite, this doesn't add them automatically — they need to sign in to Kolumn and accept the invitation before they get access. You can cancel a pending invitation at any time from the same panel.

A few things it blocks up front: you can't invite yourself, and you can't invite someone who's already a member or already has a pending invite to that board.

## What they can see

Accepting adds them to that one board only — not your other boards, and not a workspace. They can open it, see every card, and be assigned to cards on it, the same as anyone else on the board.

## Removing someone, or leaving

If you own the board, hover a member's row and click the trash icon to remove them — this is instant, with no confirmation step. You can't remove the owner (that's you). If you're a member rather than the owner, your own row shows **Leave** instead, which asks you to confirm first: "You'll lose access to this board. The owner can re-invite you later."

Either way, the moment someone's membership row is removed, their name is automatically cleared from any card on that board they were assigned to — the card doesn't keep pointing at someone who can no longer open it. See [Leave or remove someone from a board](/support/leave-or-remove-someone-from-a-board) for the full picture, including what happens to the board's owner.

## Board sharing vs. workspaces

They're two separate systems and it's worth knowing which one you're using:

- **Board sharing** (this article): invite one person to one board. No workspace involved. Good for a one-off collaborator or a client who only needs a single board.
- **Workspaces**: a container that groups several boards under one team, where every member sees every board in it. Good for an ongoing team that works across multiple boards together.

A board can only belong to one workspace or be personal — never both — but a personal board's sharing list and a workspace's member list are unrelated to each other either way.
