---
title: Anatomy of a card
---

A card has a fixed set of fields. Click any card to open it and edit all of them; a few are also editable right on the board without opening it.

## Title

The only required field. On the board, click a card to open it; the title is editable inline at the top — click the text, type, and it saves. A blank title falls back to "Untitled task."

## Description

Free text under the title. Click it to edit; click away (or press Escape while editing) to save.

## Icon

Every card shows a Phosphor icon, boxed by default. Click the icon to open a picker and choose a different one; cards with no icon set show a plain document icon instead. Clicking a card's icon on the board (outside the detail view) toggles every card on the board between the boxed style and a plain, borderless icon — a display preference, not a per-card one.

## Priority

Low, Medium, or High. In the card's detail view it shows as a colored flag — lime for low, honey for medium, copper for high — click it to change priority. On the board itself, that same color shows through the check-circle next to the title, but clicking the check-circle there marks the card complete, not a priority change (while a new card is still being created inline, that check-circle is the priority picker instead, since there's nothing to complete yet).

## Due date

Click the date pill to open a calendar and pick a day, or clear it. The pill's color tells you where you stand: copper once a date is in the past, honey on the day itself, lime for anything upcoming.

## Labels

Short tags you create per board. In the card detail view, click the dashed **Label** button (or the small plus icon next to any existing label chips), type a name, and either pick an existing label or create a new one in one of eight colors. Labels display three ways — as text, as an outlined pill, or as a color dot — and clicking any label on any card cycles the whole board through the three styles.

## Checklist

A list of sub-items with their own checkboxes, added from the card's detail view — type into the "Add an item…" field and press Enter. Each item can be ticked, edited by clicking its text, or removed; there's no drag-to-reorder, so items stay in the order you added them. The board card shows a count badge (e.g. "2/4") when a checklist exists, and clicking it expands the list right there without opening the card.

## Assignees

A card can have more than one assignee. Click the assignee circle to search board members or type a free-text name for someone who isn't a member. Assigning yourself shows your own profile icon instead of an initials avatar.

## Task number

Every card gets a number, shown as `#12` in search results. It's assigned by the server when the card is created and counts up per board — it isn't shown on the card face itself.

## Completing a card

Click the check-circle next to the title to mark a card done. A completed card's title gets a strikethrough and the check turns lime; it stays in its column — completing a card doesn't move it anywhere. Click the check again to reopen it.
