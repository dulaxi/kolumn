---
title: Add, rename, and delete columns
---

Columns show up as **sections** in Kolumn's own UI labels, but they're the same thing you'd call columns in any kanban tool — the vertical lanes cards live in.

## Add a column

At the right end of the board, click **Add section**. Type a name and press Enter (or click the confirm button next to it) to create it. Click away without typing anything and it's dropped.

## Rename a column

Click the **⋯** menu at the top of a column and choose **Rename**. The title becomes an editable field — type the new name and press Enter, or press Escape to cancel and keep the old name.

## Set a WIP limit

The same **⋯** menu has a **WIP limit** option. Set a maximum number of cards for that column; once it's full, adding another card is blocked with a warning, and the column's card count turns red while it's over the limit. Leave the field empty to remove the limit.

## Delete a column

Open the **⋯** menu and choose **Delete**. If the column has cards in it, Kolumn asks you to confirm first and tells you how many tasks will be deleted along with it — deleting a column deletes every card inside it too, there's no option to move them out first. If the column is empty, it deletes immediately.

Either way, you get the same 5-second undo as any other delete — see [Undo a delete](/support/undo-a-delete) for how that works and how long you have.
