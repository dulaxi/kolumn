Every shortcut Kolumn binds, and what ⌘K search actually searches — a reference to scan, not a walkthrough. For the workflow, see the tutorial: [Find anything with ⌘K](/tutorials/search-and-shortcuts).

## Global shortcuts

- **⌘K** / **Ctrl+K** — open search
- **/** — open search (does the same thing as ⌘K)
- **⌘B** / **Ctrl+B** — toggle the sidebar
- **Shift+?** — show or hide the shortcut list
- **Esc** — close whatever's open (a dialog, or the card panel), and blur whatever text field you're typing in
- **N** — new card, while you're viewing a board

⌘ is the modifier on macOS; every other platform uses Ctrl. `/` and `N` are ignored while a text field has focus, so typing a task title with a slash or the letter "n" in it works normally — `Esc` is the one exception, since it needs to work while you're typing in order to get you out of the field. The in-app shortcut list (Shift+?) shows the first five of these; `N` isn't on it yet, but it works.

## Inside search

Once the search dialog is open:

- **↓** / **↑** — move the highlighted result
- **Enter** — open the highlighted result
- **Esc** — close search

## What search actually searches

Search matches a card's title, its description, and its task number — typing `42` matches `#42`. It searches every board you're a member of at once, not just the one you're looking at, and it skips archived cards. Matching starts at two characters.
