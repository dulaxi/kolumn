---
title: Upgrade, cancel, or change your plan
---

Everything about your plan lives in `Settings → Billing`. Kolumn's paid billing hasn't launched yet, so this is all managed directly — no payment method is collected anywhere in the app today.

## See what you're on

The Billing pane shows your current plan and what it includes, with an **Upgrade** button if you're on Free or an **Adjust plan** button if you're on a paid plan. Either one takes you to the plan picker. The Payment and Invoices sections are honest about where things stand: there's no payment method or invoice history to show, because nothing has ever been charged.

## Move to Pro

Picking Pro from the plan picker takes you to a dedicated page with a monthly/yearly toggle and a payment section — but that section says plainly that no card is required yet, and that you'll be emailed before anything is ever charged once billing goes live. Confirming switches your account to Pro immediately, at no cost. See [Free vs Pro](/support/free-vs-pro) for what actually changes.

## Move back to Free

From `Settings → Billing`, a paid plan shows a **Cancel** button under Cancellation. Confirming moves you back to Free right away — no waiting period, no partial refund to work out, since nothing was charged in the first place. Your boards and cards aren't touched either way; only what the AI is allowed to do changes.

You can also switch plans from the picker page directly (reached via **Adjust plan**) — picking Free there does the same thing immediately, without the separate confirmation step.

## Team

Team appears in the plan picker as a real option, but it's marked **Coming soon** and its button is disabled — you can't switch to it from the app yet.
