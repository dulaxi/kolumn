---
title: Change or reset your password
---

There's one page for setting a new password, reached two different ways depending on whether you're signed in or locked out.

## If you're signed in

Go to `Settings → Account` and click **Change password**. You'll land on the password page with no extra confirmation step — since you're already signed in, it doesn't ask for your current password, just the new one twice. Enter a password of at least 6 characters, confirm it, and submit. You're updated immediately and taken back to your dashboard.

## If you're locked out

There's no "forgot password" link on the sign-in screen today — go straight to [/forgot-password](/forgot-password) and enter the email on your account. You'll get an email with a reset link; clicking it opens the same password page, where you set a new password the same way as above.

The reset email doesn't tell you whether that address has an account — it's sent the same way either way, so it can't be used to check if a particular email is signed up.

## Password requirements

Kolumn only enforces a minimum length: at least 6 characters, on both the change and reset paths.
